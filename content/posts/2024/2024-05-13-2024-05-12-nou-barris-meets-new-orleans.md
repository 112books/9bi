* Taller de paraigües decorats
* [BKT Brass Band](https://bktbrassband.com)
* **C**oncert de [Zulu Men](https://www.instagram.com/zulu_men_band/)
* DJ [Lil’ Koko](https://www.djlilkoko.com)
